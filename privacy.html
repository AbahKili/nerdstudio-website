Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Who We Are

Nerd Studio is an independent software studio operating at nerdstudio.online. We build developer tools and productivity apps. This policy covers all subdomains including pptx.nerdstudio.online, board.nerdstudio.online, and others.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our services, we may collect:

  • Account information: Email address and name when you sign in with Google OAuth
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, conversion requests made
  • Content you provide: Design URLs submitted for conversion, board names, card content

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting the NAI opt-out page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.

4. Google OAuth

Some of our applications use Google OAuth for authentication. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture. We do not access your Google Drive, Calendar, or other Google services without your explicit permission (granted through separate OAuth scopes within specific applications).

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To authenticate your identity
  • To communicate with you about your account or our services
  • To improve our products based on usage patterns

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on a private VPS server. We use industry-standard security practices including SSL/TLS encryption, firewall protection, and regular security audits. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at abah@nerdstudio.online.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at abah@nerdstudio.online.
