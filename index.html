How We Build

Made for ourselves first.

Our product philosophy is simple: if we wouldn't pay for it, we don't ship it.

Consumer-first development

We are our own first users. Every feature starts as something we personally need. No market research, no user personas — just real problems we encounter.

Ship fast, iterate faster

We release early and update often. A working prototype today beats a perfect product next quarter. Our users get improvements weekly, sometimes daily.

Collaboration is default

Sharing and teamwork aren't premium add-ons. Nerd Board has granular ReBAC. skip.my.id lets teams co-build microsites. We build for groups, not just individuals.
