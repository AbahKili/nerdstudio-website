We build for ourselves first.

Nerd Studio is an independent software studio based in Jakarta, Indonesia. We make SaaS tools that solve our own workflow problems — and open them to others who might find them useful.

What we believe

We started because we couldn't find tools that fit how we work. Claude would generate beautiful designs, but there was no clean way to turn them into Google Slides. We needed a project board that actually integrated with our Google Workspace, not just linked to it. We wanted short links with collaborative microsite features, not just redirects.

So we built them. One by one. On a quiet VPS in Singapore.

We don't have a product team, a marketing department, or a roadmap deck. We have real problems, curiosity about how to solve them, and the conviction that useful software doesn't need to be complicated.

How we operate

We're consumer-driven, not investor-driven. Every feature exists because we needed it ourselves. We ship updates weekly, answer every email, and treat our users like collaborators — not metrics on a dashboard.

Our stack is intentionally simple: Node.js, SQLite, Nginx, and a single VPS. Not because we can't use more, but because we haven't needed to. Complexity should earn its place.
